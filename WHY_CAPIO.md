# Why Capio?

The world is full of knowledge, stories, and ideas published in different
languages. Researchers explore foreign academic papers, specialists consult
technical documentation, readers discover novels from other cultures, and
curious minds follow ideas wherever they lead. Yet language barriers can turn
the simple act of reading into a constant cycle of copying, switching
applications, translating, and trying to find your place again.

Capio was built to make that process simpler.

Inside the book reader, you can translate a word or passage in place, translate
entire pages, ask AI to explain difficult material, or listen to translations
while following the text. Page-by-page translation and read aloud can also be
automated, letting you move through a book without manually repeating the same
steps.

**And Capio does not stop at books.**

The floating translator keeps a small button available while you work in other
applications. Select an area of the screen and translate its visible text using
OCR, or send the selected image directly to a supported AI vision model. This
makes it useful for translating text encountered on websites and platforms
such as YouTube, Facebook, and GitHub, as well as in other desktop applications.

Whether you are reading a novel, studying a scientific paper, exploring
unfamiliar documentation, or simply following your curiosity online, Capio
brings translation closer to the material you are trying to understand.

**Less switching between apps. Less interruption. More reading, understanding,
and discovery.**
